Small clinics miss calls. Reception is busy with someone at the desk, staff are on lunch, or the phone rings after closing. A missed call often means a patient who tries somewhere else.

That is why some clinics are trying AI phone receptionists. The idea is simple: something picks up when nobody can, and takes care of the routine bit.

I built a voice receptionist demo that answers, books into a calendar and texts a confirmation. It also hands the call to a person when it is unsure.

That handover matters to me. It should not pretend to handle everything.
