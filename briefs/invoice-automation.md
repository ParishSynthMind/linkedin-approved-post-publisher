facts:
- The firm received around 300 supplier invoices a month as PDF email attachments.
- Each invoice used to take about 4 minutes to key in by hand.
- The new flow reads the PDF, extracts the fields and puts low-confidence ones in front of a person.
- A person still approves every invoice before it reaches the accounts system.
