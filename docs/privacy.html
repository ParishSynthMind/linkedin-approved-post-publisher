Privacy policy

Last updated 5 November 2026.

Approved Post Publisher is a small open-source tool from Synthetic AI Automation. It runs only on the computer of the person who installs it, and that person uses it to post to their own LinkedIn profile. It posts only text that the person has read and approved.

  • What it accesses: with your permission, your basic LinkedIn profile (your member id and name, scopes openid and profile) and the ability to create posts on your own profile (scope w_member_social). It never reads anyone else's profile, your feed, your connections or your messages, and it never sends messages or connection requests.
  • What it posts: only drafts you have approved. Nothing is posted automatically, and an approved draft that is changed afterwards needs approving again.
  • Where data is kept: your LinkedIn access token is stored on your own computer, in the macOS Keychain (or a private file only your user can read). Drafts, the approval queue and the posted log are local files. Nothing is sent to any server run by the developer.
  • Who it is shared with: approved posts are sent only to LinkedIn. Drafts are written by Anthropic's Claude API from a brief you write; the brief and the draft are sent to Anthropic for that purpose only. Your data is never sold, rented or shared with anyone else, and it is not used for advertising.
  • Deleting your data: remove the app in LinkedIn under Settings, Data privacy, Permitted services, then delete the tool's Keychain items (names starting linkedin-) and its queue folder.
